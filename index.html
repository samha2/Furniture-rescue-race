Furniture Rescue Race — Beginner Instructions

Welcome! This short prototype teaches reuse and repair. You'll move around the workshop, collect used parts (Tops, Legs, Cushions), repair them at the Bench, assemble furniture from blueprints, and deliver it.

  • Move: Arrow keys (or WASD).
  • Interact / pick up / use bench / deliver: Press E.
  • Open the bench when near it to repair and assemble (use your mouse to select items in the bench UI).

Goal in challenge mode: Deliver 3 assembled furniture items within 5 minutes.

Tip: If you get stuck, parts will respawn automatically so you can always progress.

How it teaches reuse

Collect used parts around the workshop, repair them at the bench, and assemble furniture. Repairs save materials (you'll see "Saved" points) and reduce waste. In challenge mode aim to complete orders quickly; in relaxed mode practice repairs and assembly without a clock.

Beginner-friendly prototype — easy to extend.
