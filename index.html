Game Modes

How it teaches reuse

Collect used parts around the workshop, repair them at the bench, and assemble furniture using blueprints. Repairs save materials (you'll see "Saved" points) and reduce waste. In challenge mode aim to complete orders quickly; in relaxed mode play without a timer to practice repair and assembly.

Beginner-friendly prototype — easy to extend.
